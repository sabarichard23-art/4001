Privacy Policy

400 card game. Last updated November 21, 2026

The short version

The game has no ads and no tracking, and it does not sell or share personal information. You can play against bots without giving any information. If you set up a profile to add friends, the name, username and picture you choose are stored so friends can find and invite you.

What stays on your device

  • Your scores, your win and loss record, your chosen bot levels and the name you type for the table.
  • A random identifier used only to keep your chair if your connection drops during a game with friends.

This is stored in the app's local storage. Deleting the app, or clearing site data in your browser, removes it.

Profiles and friends

The game creates an anonymous account for your device, with no email, phone number or password. If you set up a profile, these are stored with Google Firebase (Google LLC): the name and username you enter, the picture you pick from the built-in set, your friends list and friend requests, and table invites you send or receive. Other signed-in players can see your name, username and picture.

You can delete your account at any time: open your profile in the game and choose Delete my account. This removes your profile, username, friends list and invites.

Playing with friends

When you open or join a table, your device exchanges game messages with the other players through public message relay servers (broker.emqx.io, broker.hivemq.com and test.mosquitto.org). These messages contain the table code, the name you typed, your chair, your bids and card plays, and any emote or message you send. They are passed along as they happen. The game does not store them. The relay operators handle connections, including IP addresses, under their own policies.

Use a nickname at the table if you prefer not to share your real name.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
